# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — contributors and maintainers of SetuExam — pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, caste, or sexual orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the project:
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the mission.
- Attacking ideas, not people.

Behaviour that hurts the project:
- Personal attacks, harassment, doxxing.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Communal, casteist, or political slurs.
- Publishing others' private information (real names, contact info, workplace) without consent.
- Deliberate misinformation intended to derail or discredit contributors.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and PRs that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

To report unacceptable behaviour, open a GitHub Issue tagged `code-of-conduct` or contact the maintainers privately. All reports will be reviewed and investigated confidentially.
