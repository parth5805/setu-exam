## 4. Political / Policy Signal

Educator-endorsed technological reforms being discussed:
- **End-to-end encryption** of question papers in transit and at rest
- **AI-based monitoring** of exam centres and question distribution
- **Blockchain-backed document trails** for tamper-evident audit
- **Secure digital paper transmission** (replacing physical printing/transport)
- **Computer-Based Testing (CBT)** wherever feasible

Direction: from the next exam cycle the Ministry of Education is expected to push CBT + digital security across all major public exams (NTA, UPSC, SSC, state PSCs).

## 5. Why This Project

Build a **prototype blockchain-native architecture** that a serious government/testing-agency stakeholder could pilot. Not a whitepaper — a working reference implementation covering:
- Question-paper lifecycle (authoring → sealing → distribution → decryption → destruction)
- Candidate identity (fake candidates / impersonation is a large fraction of leaks)
- Auditable trails without leaking content
- Center-level controls and monitoring
